# Charter

> [!NOTE]
> This is an English translation of the Japanese original, [`CHARTER.md`](CHARTER.md). If there is any discrepancy in interpretation between the Japanese and English versions, the Japanese version shall prevail.

## 1. Principles and use
PHOTON GLASS is a metamedium whose purpose is to support people in perceiving the world, accessing information and understanding. Its design is based on the principle of supporting the autonomy and dignity of its users.

A device equipped with a first-person camera and a network connection may be diverted from its original purpose to acts that infringe the privacy or rights of others. Photographing or recording without consent, acts that undermine the fairness of examinations or other assessments, and acts that violate laws and regulations are incompatible with the principles of this project. PHOTON GLASS is not intended to be developed or used for such acts.

Anything made contrary to these principles may not use the name PHOTON GLASS (including "PHOTON GLASS made in XX").

## 2. The name
PHOTON GLASS is a metamedium defined by the condition that it "interacts with the user through feedback such as sound, based on images captured by a first-person camera".

If what you make is almost the same as what is published here, please use the name PHOTON GLASS. The same applies if you make it with updated data, or with the parts you source changed to suit local circumstances. When it is made in a particular region, we recommend the form "PHOTON GLASS made in XX" (where XX is the name of the region).

If you modify its functions for a different purpose, please give it a new name. In that case, please state in the credits that it is based on PHOTON GLASS.

The provisions of this Charter concerning the name are not licence conditions; they set out the policy of the project.

## 3. Patents
PHOTON GLASS includes technology that constitutes the working of Japanese Patent No. 7626915, "読み上げ装置" (read-aloud device; patent holder: OTON GLASS Inc.). OTON GLASS Inc. will not exercise any rights under this patent against those who use PHOTON GLASS in compliance with its respective licences.

This declaration is intended to ensure that users who comply with the licences are not hindered by the patent; it does not waive the patent itself.
